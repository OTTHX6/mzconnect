Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

How do I book a service?

Search for a service, compare professionals, choose your provider and confirm your booking.

Are service providers verified?

Yes. All professionals go through an identity and qualification verification process before joining the platform.

How do payments work?

Customers can pay through supported payment methods including M-Pesa, E-Mola, cards or cash.

Can I leave a review?

Absolutely. After each completed service, customers can rate and review providers.

What if I need urgent help?

Emergency service categories allow customers to request immediate assistance from available professionals nearby.
